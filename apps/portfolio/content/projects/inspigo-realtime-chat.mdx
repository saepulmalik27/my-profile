## Problem

Live classes and community cohorts needed real-time Q&A and chat during
the pandemic-driven surge in livestreaming demand, at a time when
browser support for `getUserMedia` was inconsistent across devices.

## Architecture

Agora handled WebRTC voice and video; Firebase stored user-behavior
analytics alongside the chat and community layer.

## Execution

Built with a team. The main hard problem was cross-device and
cross-browser compatibility in 2022, when audio/video support through
`getUserMedia` wasn't reliably available everywhere yet.

## Impact

Saw high usage at the time, during a period of very high demand for
livestreaming-driven products. This feature has since been taken down
from the Inspigo product and no longer exists — no screenshots or
recordings are available.
