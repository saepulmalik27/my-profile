## Problem

No SEO work existed at Inspigo before this — proactive, not a fix for a
known problem.

## Architecture

Technical SEO built and monitored with Lighthouse and SEMrush alongside
Google Search Console.

![Lighthouse audit](/project/SEO/light-house.png)

![Search Console](/project/SEO/search-console.png)

![SEMrush](/project/SEO/semrush.png)

## Execution

Technical SEO alone wasn't enough to move rankings — metadata and
keyword strategy had to be layered on top before rankings actually
improved, not just technical audit scores.

## Impact

Core Web Vitals are green across the board. "Inspigo" as a branded
search term ranks #1 domestically; individual product pages are mixed,
with some landing in the top 10 and others still climbing.
