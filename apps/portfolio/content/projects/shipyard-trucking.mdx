## Problem

Shipyard project workflows and trucking logistics both ran on manual,
fragmented processes — project status tracked outside any single system,
and truck fleets coordinated without real-time visibility.

## Architecture

PHP backends (Laravel, CodeIgniter, and CakePHP, depending on the
project) paired with Vue.js frontends. The trucking system layered in
GPS tracking and analytics on top of the same full-stack foundation.

## Execution

Built the Shipyard Project Management System to cover the workflow
end-to-end — from project intake through to completion, in one place
instead of scattered across spreadsheets and conversations. Maintained
and improved the Trucking Management Application's GPS tracking and
analytics as fleet operations grew.

## Impact

Operations teams gained a single system to track shipyard projects and
truck fleets in real time instead of relying on manual coordination —
full-stack range across backend, frontend, and real-time data that later
underpinned the move into frontend and AI specialization at Inspigo.
