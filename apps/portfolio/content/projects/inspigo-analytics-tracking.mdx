## Problem

The product needed visibility into user behavior and campaign
triggering, and a way to catch errors early — without building
everything from raw GTM/GA tooling, which gets unwieldy for
journey-level tracking.

## Architecture

Google Analytics and Facebook Pixel both route through Google Tag
Manager. MoEngage handles campaign triggering off real user behavior.
User-journey tracking specifically relies on MoEngage plus an in-house
system, **inspigo-telemetry**, built to be easier to work with for that
purpose than raw GTM/GA.

## Execution

Full owner of this stack end to end — events, user journeys, and
campaign wiring.

## Impact

Drives retention and engagement analysis, gives visibility into
product impact through user-journey data, and enables early error
detection.
