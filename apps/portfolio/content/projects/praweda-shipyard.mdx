## Problem

Some tooling already existed, but it was scattered and fragmented — part
of the process was still recorded manually, outside any single system.
Project status, warehouse inventory, and inquiry/quotation records lived
across disconnected tools and paper trails rather than one source of
truth.

## Architecture

Built from the ground up on Laravel, Vue.js, and PostgreSQL — a
greenfield system rather than an extension of what existed before.

## Execution

Two hard problems shaped the build: syncing the manually-recorded data
that already existed into the new system without losing history, and
driving actual field adoption among shipyard staff who were used to the
old, scattered way of working.

## Impact

Faster project record-keeping, with all projects and warehouse
inventory tracked centrally. Inquiry and quotation processes are now
clearly logged in-system, closing off a prior avenue for fraud and
manipulation.
